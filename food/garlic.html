How long does garlic last?

Garlic

As bought, in the cupboardabout 3 months
Once cut3 days

Opening never extends a date: if the packet date comes sooner, go by that. Estimates for quality, not a safety ruling. What the dates mean

Garlic keeps for about 3 months in the cupboard, counted from when you buy it. That's SnapLarder's estimate for garlic in general. If the packet has a date, go by the packet: it's specific to that product.

There's no need to freeze garlic.

How long does fresh salsa last?

Fresh salsa

As bought, in the fridge5 days
Once opened5 days

Opening never extends a date: if the packet date comes sooner, go by that. Estimates for quality, not a safety ruling. What the dates mean

Fresh salsa keeps for 5 days in the fridge, counted from when you buy it. That's SnapLarder's estimate for fresh salsa in general. If the packet has a date, go by the packet: it's specific to that product.

Fresh salsa doesn't freeze well.

How long does rocket last?

Rocket

As bought, in the fridge5 days
Once cut3 days

Opening never extends a date: if the packet date comes sooner, go by that. Estimates for quality, not a safety ruling. What the dates mean

Rocket keeps for 5 days in the fridge, counted from when you buy it. That's SnapLarder's estimate for rocket (arugula) in general. If the packet has a date, go by the packet: it's specific to that product.

Rocket doesn't freeze well.

Let your phone keep track

Scan the barcode or photograph the label. SnapLarder dates it, moves the date when you open or freeze it, and reminds you before it goes off.

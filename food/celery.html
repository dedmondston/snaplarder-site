How long does celery last?

Celery

As bought, in the fridgeabout 2 weeks
Once cut3 days

Opening never extends a date: if the packet date comes sooner, go by that. Estimates for quality, not a safety ruling. What the dates mean

Celery keeps for about 2 weeks in the fridge, counted from when you buy it. That's SnapLarder's estimate for celery in general. If the packet has a date, go by the packet: it's specific to that product.

Celery doesn't freeze well.

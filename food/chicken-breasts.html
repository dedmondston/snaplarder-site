How long do chicken breasts last?

Chicken breasts

As bought, in the fridge4 days
Once opened2 days
In the freezerabout 6 months
After thawinguse within 2 days

Opening never extends a date: if the packet date comes sooner, go by that. Estimates for quality, not a safety ruling. What the dates mean

Chicken breasts keep for 4 days in the fridge, counted from when you buy them. That's SnapLarder's estimate for chicken breasts in general. If the packet has a date, go by the packet: it's specific to that product.

A use-by date is about safety. Don’t eat food after its use-by date, even if it looks and smells fine.

Let your phone keep track

Scan the barcode or photograph the label. SnapLarder dates it, moves the date when you open or freeze it, and reminds you before it goes off.

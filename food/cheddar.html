How long does cheddar last?

Cheddar

As bought, in the fridgeabout 6 weeks
In the freezerabout 3 months
After thawinguse within 2 days

Estimates for quality, not a safety ruling. What the dates mean

Cheddar keeps for about 6 weeks in the fridge, counted from when you buy it. That's SnapLarder's estimate for cheddar in general. If the packet has a date, go by the packet: it's specific to that product.

A use-by date is about safety. Don’t eat food after its use-by date, even if it looks and smells fine.

How long do hot dog sausages last?

Hot dog sausages

As bought, in the fridgeabout 2 weeks
Once opened2 days
In the freezerabout 6 months
After thawinguse within 2 days

Opening never extends a date: if the packet date comes sooner, go by that. Estimates for quality, not a safety ruling. What the dates mean

Hot dog sausages keep for about 2 weeks in the fridge, counted from when you buy them. That's SnapLarder's estimate for hot dog sausages in general. If the packet has a date, go by the packet: it's specific to that product.

A use-by date is about safety. Don’t eat food after its use-by date, even if it looks and smells fine.

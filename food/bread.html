How long does bread last?

Bread

As bought, in the cupboard6 days
In the freezerabout 6 months
After thawinguse within 2 days

Estimates for quality, not a safety ruling. What the dates mean

Bread keeps for 6 days in the cupboard, counted from when you buy it. That's SnapLarder's estimate for bread in general. If the packet has a date, go by the packet: it's specific to that product.

Once opened, follow the storage instructions on the label. Some products need to go in the fridge after opening.

Let your phone keep track

SnapLarder works this out for you. Scan the barcode or photograph the label, and it dates each item, then moves the date when you open or freeze it. You get a reminder before it goes off.

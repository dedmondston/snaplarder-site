How long does pineapple last?

Pineapple

As bought, in the cupboard5 days
Once cut3 days
In the freezerabout 8 months
After thawinguse within 2 days

Opening never extends a date: if the packet date comes sooner, go by that. Estimates for quality, not a safety ruling. What the dates mean

Pineapple keeps for 5 days in the cupboard, counted from when you buy it. That's SnapLarder's estimate for pineapple in general. If the packet has a date, go by the packet: it's specific to that product.

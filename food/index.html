How long does food last?

Typical times for 113 everyday foods, in the fridge, freezer and cupboard. Tap a food for opened and frozen times.

Use-by or best before? A use-by date is about safety: don't eat food after it, even if it looks and smells fine. A best-before date is about quality. These times are general estimates; when the packet has a date, go by the packet. More on date labels · US date labels

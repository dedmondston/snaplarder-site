How long does food last?

Typical times for 104 everyday foods, in the fridge, the freezer and the cupboard.

Use-by or best before? A use-by date is about safety: don't eat food after it, even if it looks and smells fine. A best-before date is about quality: food is usually fine after it, but may not be at its best. These times are general estimates. When the packet has a date, go by the packet.

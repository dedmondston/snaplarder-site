How long does olive oil last?

Olive oil

As bought, in the cupboardabout a year

Estimates for quality, not a safety ruling. What the dates mean

Olive oil keeps for about a year in the cupboard, counted from when you buy it. That's SnapLarder's estimate for olive oil in general. If the packet has a date, go by the packet: it's specific to that product.

Once opened, follow the storage instructions on the label. Some products need to go in the fridge after opening.

There's no need to freeze olive oil.

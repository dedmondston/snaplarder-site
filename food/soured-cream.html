How long does soured cream (sour cream) last?

Soured cream

As bought, in the fridgeabout 3 weeks
Once opened10 days

Opening never extends a date: if the packet date comes sooner, go by that. Estimates for quality, not a safety ruling. What the dates mean

Soured cream keeps for about 3 weeks in the fridge, counted from when you buy it. That's SnapLarder's estimate for soured cream (sour cream) in general. If the packet has a date, go by the packet: it's specific to that product.

A use-by date is about safety. Don’t eat food after its use-by date, even if it looks and smells fine. Use by vs best before

Can you freeze soured cream?

Not really. Soured cream doesn't freeze well: the texture changes when it thaws.

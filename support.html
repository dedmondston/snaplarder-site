Support

SnapLarder is made by one person. I read everything that comes in, and I usually reply within a week.

Reporting something that went wrong? These three things let me fix it without a back-and-forth:

1. What you expected to happen
2. What happened instead
3. The product name, if a scan was involved

Send a report

Or email support@snaplarder.com.

A barcode wasn't recognised

SnapLarder looks products up in Open Food Facts, a free public database. It's good but not complete, and own-brand lines are often missing. You can still photograph the label, or type the item in.

If you'd like a product added, anyone can add it at openfoodfacts.org — it then works for everyone.

Something landed in the wrong place

Tap the item and change the fridge, freezer or cupboard chip. If a product is consistently wrong, tell me its name and barcode and I'll correct it for everyone in the next update.

My reminders aren't arriving

Check Settings inside SnapLarder first — reminders can be switched off there, and it will warn you if iPhone-level notifications are off. If both look right, check iPhone Settings → Notifications → SnapLarder.

I paid but the app still shows a limit

Settings → Purchase → Restore purchase. It re-checks with Apple using the same Apple ID that bought it. If that doesn't work, email me.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by me. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple ID that made the purchase.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything SnapLarder knows is stored on your phone and goes with it. There's no account and no copy anywhere else.

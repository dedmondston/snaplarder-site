In one line

SnapLarder is an iPhone app that tracks what's in your fridge, freezer and cupboard, and reminds you before food goes off.

In a paragraph

SnapLarder helps households waste less food. Scan a barcode, photograph a label or type a name, and the app dates each item, shows what to use first, and sends a reminder before it goes off. Open or freeze something and the date updates with it. There's no account and nothing to sign up for: your food list stays on your phone. It's free to try, then a one-off unlock removes the limit, with no subscription.

Key facts

  • Platform: iPhone (App Store, UK, Ireland, US, Australia and New Zealand)
  • Price: free for 14 days with no limits, then 10 items free or a one-off unlock
  • Privacy: no account, no adverts, no tracking; food data stays on the phone
  • Adding food: barcode scan, photo of the label or receipt, or type it in
  • Food guide: free shelf-life guide for over 100 foods at snaplarder.com/food
  • Made by: an independent developer in Essex, UK

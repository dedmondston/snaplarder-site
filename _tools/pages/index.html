Most trackers ask when something expires. SnapLarder asks what you did with it.

  • Three ways in

    Scan barcode for packets, Photo of label for printed dates and receipts, Type it in for loose fruit and veg. A whole shop takes about a minute.

  • Dates you can check

    Every date says where it came from — read off the label or estimated — and you can change any of them in two taps.

  • Reminders that suit you

    Choose how many days' notice and what time of day, so the nudge arrives when you can still act on it.

  • Nothing leaves your phone

    No account, no server holding your shopping. A barcode lookup sends the number and nothing else. Privacy policy

  • One payment

    Free for a fortnight with no limits, then ten items free, or unlock unlimited once. Nothing to cancel.

  • New to it?

    A short guide shows you around the first time. See it again any time from Settings, or get help here.

SnapLarder is a reminder tool, not a food safety product. It estimates dates from shelf-life tables and from what's printed on the packet. It cannot tell you whether food is safe to eat — use your own judgement, and when in doubt, throw it out. Read the food safety notice.

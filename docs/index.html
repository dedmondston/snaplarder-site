SnapLarder

Know before it goes off.

Download on the App Store

WHAT IT DOES

  • Scan a barcode or photograph a label — it fills in the rest
  • Reminds you a few days before something expires, at a time you choose
  • Knows the difference between sealed and opened, fridge and freezer
  • Everything stays on your phone

PRICE

Free for 14 days with no limits. After that you can keep tracking 10 items free, or unlock unlimited items with a one-off payment. It isn't a subscription and there's nothing to cancel.

A note on food safety. SnapLarder is a reminder tool. It estimates dates from shelf-life tables and from what's printed on the packet — it cannot tell you whether food is safe to eat. Use your own judgement, and when in doubt, throw it out.
